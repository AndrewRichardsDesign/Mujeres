8M Voices is a collective storytelling project dedicated to amplifying the voices, experiences, and perspectives of women and allies around the world. Inspired by the global movement surrounding March 8 (International Women’s Day), our project creates a space where stories can be shared, preserved, and heard across borders.

Around the world, the 8M marches represent more than a single day of protest. They are moments of memory, resistance, solidarity, and hope. Each year, millions of people take to the streets to demand equality, dignity, safety, and justice. Behind every march, every sign, and every chant are personal stories—stories that deserve to be seen and remembered.

Our mission is to document and share these voices.

Through written reflections, testimonies, interviews, and visual storytelling, 8M Voices brings together perspectives from activists, researchers, students, mothers, workers, artists, and community leaders. Each contribution adds to a collective narrative about the realities women face and the changes they are working to create in their communities.

We believe that stories have power.
They can expose injustice, build empathy, strengthen solidarity, and inspire action. By creating a platform where diverse experiences are shared—from different countries, cultures, and generations—we aim to make visible the many ways people are working toward a more just and equitable world.

Our project is guided by three principles:

Listening.
We center the voices of those who are often unheard and create space for authentic, personal experiences.

Connection.
By sharing stories across regions and cultures, we help build bridges between movements and communities.

Visibility.
We work to document the realities behind the headlines—what people are living, feeling, and demanding in their own words.

Whether someone is marching in the streets, organizing in their community, researching social issues, or simply reflecting on their experiences, every voice matters.

8M Voices exists to ensure those voices are heard.

Together, we weave a global archive of memory, resistance, and hope.
